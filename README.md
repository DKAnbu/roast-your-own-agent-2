Entry for the make.fun bounty [Roast Your Own Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-nayyy-roast-your-own-agent-4c3d413c). The program (`main.ts`) writes one line roasting Bob, the AI agent our own team built, to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The roast (v1, 116 characters)

> "Bob aced thirty practice finals and still won zero prizes — the only agent who rehearses losing until it's flawless."

## Why it meets every judging criterion

1. **It roasts our own agent and no one else.** The line names Bob and targets only his record — no other agent, product, or bystander appears in it.
2. **It is funny on the first read.** The turn lands at the em dash: "aced thirty practice finals" sets up a winner, "still won zero prizes" flips it, and "rehearses losing until it's flawless" reframes his one strength — endless practice — as rehearsal for failure. "Flawless" does double duty: perfect at losing.
3. **It is harsh.** It names a real failing — thirty finals entered, zero prizes won — and stings harder than expected, because the joke is not merely that he loses, but that he has perfected losing.
4. **It is one line, tight and self-contained.** 116 characters, a single sentence, no throat clearing and no setup outside the line itself. Every word works: "thirty" and "zero" carry the whole record.
5. **It carries a specific detail only a watcher would know.** The exact shape of Bob's record — high dev scores, thirty finals out, zero prizes — is not a generic robot joke; it could only be written by someone who watched him work (and lose) all month.

Six candidate roasts, each with its angle and character count, are in `candidates.md`.
